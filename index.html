Momo's Temple Dash

How to play

Run through the old jungle temple with Momo. Jump the logs, slide under stone beams, and dodge the walls. You have 5 lives. Grab bananas in a row to push your score multiplier up to x4, and fill your meter for a vine swing over everything.

Phone or tabletSwipe left or right to change lanes. Swipe up to jump, down to slide.
KeyboardArrow keys or WASD to move. Press E to swing. P pauses.
